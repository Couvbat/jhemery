THIRD-PARTY NOTICES — jhemery.xyz
=================================

This file travels with the deployed site (it is served at
https://jhemery.xyz/THIRD-PARTY.txt) as well as living in the repository, because
the licences below require their notices to accompany the copy that is actually
distributed.

Source code for this site: https://github.com/Couvbat/jhemery

Everything here concerns the generated word lists used by the terminal's
`wordle`, `hangman` and `wpm` games, which ship as
`assets/words-en-*.js` and `assets/words-fr-*.js`. They are produced by
`frontend/scripts/build-wordlists.mjs` from the sources below and committed to
`frontend/src/terminal/games/data/`.

Nothing else in the bundle is derived from third-party data. Runtime
dependencies (Vue, Three.js, Tailwind, and the rest) carry their own licences in
`node_modules` and are listed in `frontend/package.json`.
